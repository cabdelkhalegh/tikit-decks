Campaign Workflow

Step-by-step — from deck to live posts

🗓️ Campaign Status Pipeline

Shortlisted→ Contacted→ Confirmed→ Box Shipped→ Delivered→ Posted ✓→ Completed

Also tracked: Follow-Up 1/2 · Declined · No Response · Did Not Post · Delivery Failed

1

Share Deck + Creator Review with Client

Send Bioderma both links: the presentation deck for campaign overview, and the client approval tracker to review creators.

  • 📎 Deck: bioderma-xdefense-2026.html
  • 📋 Client tracker: bioderma-tracker/?view=client
  • 📋 Creator review: bioderma-xdefense-master-review.html (all 95 — one link with tabs by batch)
2

Client Reviews & Approves Creators

Bioderma opens the client tracker. For each creator they see the name, handle, profile links, batch, category, and reach. They click ✅ Approve or ❌ Reject — every click saves instantly to the database. TiKiT sees approvals in real time on the agency tracker.

  • Client can filter by batch, category, or approval state
  • Client can export their shortlist as CSV (⬇ Export My Shortlist)
  • SLA: client approvals within 48 hours of receiving the link
3

TiKiT Starts Outreach — Selected Reviewers

On the agency tracker, filter by "✅ Approved by Client" to see the shortlist. Send the initial DM to each creator using the "Selected Reviewer" outreach templates below. Frame it as: "You've been hand-selected as a product reviewer." The ask: 1 UGC video (honest review). Update their status to Contacted by clicking the 📤 Contact button on their row.

  • Day 0: Initial DM → status = Contacted
  • Day 3: No reply → Follow-Up 1
  • Day 7: No reply → Follow-Up 2
  • Day 10: Archive → status = No Response
4

Creator Accepts → Collect Details

When a creator agrees: click ✅ Accept button → status becomes Confirmed. Click on the row to expand and fill in their shipping address. Log the contacted_by field with your name.

5

Ship the PR Box

Dispatch the box → click 📦 Ship button → status becomes Dispatched. Enter the courier tracking number in the expanded row. When delivered, click 📬 Delivered.

  • SLA: dispatch within 48h of receiving address
  • SLA: UAE delivery in 2 business days (Aramex/Fetchr)
6

UGC Video Goes Live → Add Link

When a creator posts their 1 UGC video review, click 🎬 Posted and expand their row to paste the content link (TikTok or Instagram Reel). The client view shows these links as clickable ▶ Post 1. Click 🏁 Complete when the deliverable is done.

⚠️ Gentle Nudge Rule — Do Not Break

  1. Day 3 after delivery: "Hope the box arrived safely! No pressure at all 😊"
  2. Never ask "when will you post?" or "have you tried it yet?"
  3. If they post later than expected — celebrate it. Never complain.

How to Use the Tracker

Full feature walkthrough — agency and client views

⚙️ Agency View (Full Access)

  • Stats bar — live counts: Total, Contacted, Confirmed, Shipped, Posted, Client Approved
  • Filters — filter by Batch (B1/B2/Emirati), Status, Client Approval, Category, or search by name/handle
  • Creator row — shows: name, reach, handles (clickable IG/TT links), batch, category, campaign status, client approval badge, fee, content links
  • Quick action buttons — 📤 Contact, ✅ Accept, ❌ Decline, 📦 Ship, 📬 Delivered, 🎬 Posted, 🏁 Complete
  • Click any row — expands inline edit panel: outreach info, shipping address, tracking number, dates, 4 content link slots, internal notes
  • Client Approval column — shows ✅ Approved / ❌ Rejected / ⏳ Pending per creator based on client's selections
  • Export CSV — downloads full data with all columns
  • + Add Creator — manually add a creator not in the original list

👁️ Client View (Bioderma)

  • Shortlist summary bar — shows how many approved / rejected / pending at a glance
  • Per-creator approval buttons — ✅ Approve or ❌ Reject. Saves instantly to database. Green row = approved, red = rejected.
  • Filter — by batch, approval state, category, or search
  • Handles are clickable — 📸 IG and 🎵 TT buttons open creator profiles in new tab
  • Content links appear automatically — once TiKiT logs video links, client sees ▶ Post 1 / ▶ Post 2 etc.
  • Export My Shortlist — downloads approved creators as CSV
  • No sensitive data — fees, tracking numbers, internal notes are hidden
  • No editing — client can only approve/reject and export

📊 Approval Summary Bar (both views)

Both the agency and client views show a live summary at the top: how many creators are approved, rejected, or still pending review. This updates the moment anyone clicks an approval button — no refresh needed.

The agency stats bar also shows a "✅ Client OK" counter so TiKiT can see at a glance how many creators have been signed off on.

Outreach Templates

Copy-ready DMs — English + Arabic. Selected Reviewer Program — 1 UGC Video.

📌 Usage Rules Before Sending

  1. Always personalize — replace [Name] with their first name only. Never "Dear Influencer."
  2. Match their language — check their recent content. Arabic creator → Arabic message.
  3. Send timing — 10 AM–1 PM or 7 PM–10 PM Dubai time. Never Friday midday or late night.
  4. Instagram DM first — if they're primarily TikTok, try IG first anyway (higher open rate). TikTok DM as backup.
  5. This is a reviewer selection — frame it as "you've been hand-selected as a product reviewer." The ask is 1 UGC video (honest review). Never say "no obligation" or "post if you want."
